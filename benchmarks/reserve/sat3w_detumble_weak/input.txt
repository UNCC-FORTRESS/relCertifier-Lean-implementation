# THE SPACECRAFT (both sides; a ~60 kg microsatellite, SI units; the plant of
# sat3w_detumble_nominal at the design-model level). Body rates w = (w1, w2, w3) (rad/s), inertia
# J = diag(2, 4, 5) kg m^2, momenta h = (h1, h2, h3) (N m s) of three reaction wheels on the
# principal axes, each absorbing its axis rate with gain k_i: w_i' = -k_i w_i, h_i' = k_i J_i w_i.
# The gyroscopic and wheel couplings are not modeled on either side (they do no work; the
# refinement step that adds them is sat3w_detumble_nominal). Energy E = 2 w1^2 + 4 w2^2 + 5 w3^2.
# PHYSICAL DOMAIN (both sides): |w_i| <= 1 rad/s, |h_i| <= 2 N m s.
# Control period: 1 s on BOTH sides (epsilon = 1.0), the attitude mode manager's cycle on a 1 Hz
# ADCS (CubeSpace CubeStar attitude update 1 Hz, CubeSpace sensor data sheet).
# Guards: DETUMBLE / the three profiles engage at E <= 1; the design model's FDIR SAFE sink at
# E > 1 (frozen). The right guards cover the domain.
#
# Degraded deployed (left): the z-wheel's motor driver is current-limited to 80 % torque,
# k_3 = 0.4 /s, while the x- and y-wheels run at the nominal gain 0.5 /s:
# w1' = -0.5 w1, w2' = -0.5 w2, w3' = -0.4 w3, h1' = 1.0 w1, h2' = 2.0 w2, h3' = 2.0 w3. The
# deployed's energy then decays at a rate between -0.8 E (all energy on z) and -1.0 E (none on z).
# The design model (right) offers the ground-selectable profiles NOMINAL k = 0.5, ECONOMY 0.45 and
# SURVIVAL 0.42 on all three wheels (NOMINAL <-> ECONOMY / SURVIVAL); each declares SAFE.
# One cause between the sides: the z-wheel's torque limit.
#
# CLAIM: E_L <= E_R + 0.125, the residual-rate requirement of sat_detumble_weak (the energy of a
# 0.25 rad/s spin about the minor axis x, the sun-acquisition capture range). The lower row of
# sat_detumble_weak (E_R <= E_L, never faster than the design) is NOT part of this claim: with
# healthy x- and y-wheels the deployed detumbles those axes faster than ECONOMY and SURVIVAL, so
# that row is not a property of this spacecraft against a power-saving profile.
# Measured (2026-10-26): tightest 0.1112 (0.1111 declines), the ECONOMY bound 1 - 0.4/0.45 = 1/9,
# the same as sat_detumble_weak; under RELCERT_NO_CHOICE=1 the tightest is 0.20 (0.19 declines),
# the NOMINAL bound 1 - 0.4/0.5. With the lower row E_R <= E_L added (the two-sided claim of
# sat_detumble_weak) the file DECLINES at 0.125 and certifies only from 0.20 (committed to
# NOMINAL), so the residual-rate requirement is not shown in the two-sided form.
# Why the choice (X): a design run starting in NOMINAL must be committed to a power-saving
# profile (NOMINAL's tangent bound 0.2 exceeds 0.125). Why the cut (I): the engagement envelope
# E <= 1 bounds the energy on which the gap can grow.
#
