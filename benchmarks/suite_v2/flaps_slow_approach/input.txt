# flaps_slow_approach: a twin-jet airliner extending its flaps from CONF 1+F to CONF 2 on approach with
# one of the two hydraulic systems of the flap drive lost (FLAPS SLOW: the flaps move at half speed),
# against the same extension with both systems; the claim compares the approach speed at equal flap
# positions, i.e. against the normal extension re-timed by the flap drive's speed ratio.
# scenario: degraded-actuator
# mechanisms: S (a time stretch per mode: 1/2 while the flaps move, 1 once they are set; RELCERT_SYNC=1
#   declines; the claim is a tolerance claim, not an exact time scaling: the flap rows are exact at
#   the stretch, the speed row is not; matrix code M1)
#
# System (both sides). Flap angle f in deg, calibrated airspeed V in kt, time in s; the flap/slat
# control and the autothrust re-decide every 0.1 s (epsilon 0.1 on both sides). At f = 10 deg (CONF
# 1+F, A320 family) the crew selects CONF 2 (flaps 15 deg) at the S speed; the managed speed target
# drops to the F speed and the autothrust decelerates toward it with idle thrust; flap drag makes the
# deceleration grow with the flap angle: V' = -(0.02 + 0.001 f) (V - F), about 1.05 kt/s at f = 10 and
# 35 kt above target (the usual 1 kt/s level-flight deceleration), F = 150 kt. Two modes:
#   EXT    f < 15    the flap power control unit drives the flaps toward the detent at a constant rate
#   CONF2  f >= 15   the flaps are set (f' = 0)
# EXT -> [EXT, CONF2], CONF2 -> [CONF2]. Representative speeds for a mid landing weight, from the
# Airbus definitions (Safety First #24, July 2017: S = 1.21 to 1.23 x VS1G clean, F = 1.38 to 1.47 x
# VS1G CONF 2): S = 185 kt, F = 150 kt (VS1G clean about 152, CONF 2 about 106 kt). Flap rate 1 deg/s
# with both systems (a representative value, assumed: about 5 s for the 5 deg leg).
# Right (reference): both hydraulic systems, the flap PCU's two hydraulic motors drive the flaps at
#   1 deg/s.
# Left (deployed): one hydraulic system lost; the PCU runs on one motor and the flaps move at half
#   speed, 0.5 deg/s (A320 family FCOM, flaps and slats: with one of the two hydraulic systems of a
#   PCU the surfaces operate at half speed). Same aircraft, same autothrust, same selection. The
#   flap drive speed is the only difference.
#
# Property (both modes): f_L = f_R (two inequalities), V_R <= V_L + 25 and V_L <= V_R + 10 (kt),
# against the normal extension re-timed by a stretch in [1/2, 1] (lambda_min 0.5 = one motor of two,
# lambda_max 1). Reading: at every flap position the FLAPS SLOW aircraft is at most 25 kt slower than
# the normal one and at most 10 kt faster. Both constants are tight (bisection to 0.01, measured
# 2026-10-29: 24.99 and 9.99 decline) and both are what the autothrust's approach to F allows from
# every start the placards admit: at the stretch 1/2 the flap rows are exact and the speed gap
# D = V_R - V_L obeys D' = -k(f) D + (1/2) k(f) (V_R - F), so the gap is driven toward half the
# reference's distance from its target. From a selection at the flap placard VFE CONF 2 = 200 kt the
# slower aircraft may lag by (200 - F) / 2 = 25 kt; from a selection below the target (both speeds
# then rise toward F, the slower aircraft for twice as long per degree of flap) it may lead by
# (F - VLS) / 2 = (150 - 130) / 2 = 10 kt.
# Requirement: the speed windows operators accept around an approach speed target are 20 kt wide
# (the Airbus AFS lets the speed vary +-20 kt around the managed target, Safety First #24; the
# stabilized-approach criterion of the Flight Safety Foundation, ALAR Briefing Note 7.1, is a 20 kt
# window above VREF). The 20 kt window holds for every selection at most 40 kt above F: with the
# placard at 190 kt the rows certify at 20.00 (19.99 declines), and the procedure's selection speed
# S = 185 kt lies in that range; the placard admits selections up to 200 kt, from which the gap may
# reach 25 kt, so the claim states 25 (the domain audit of 2026-10-29: until then the domain was the
# band [F, S] = [150, 185] the closed loop never leaves, and the rows V_L <= V_R <= V_L + 20 certified
# on it with the tight value 17.50 = (1 - 1/2) (S - F); that band was an autothrust target, not a
# limit, and the rows stood on it: with it widened by 5 % the file declined). The drag coupling does
# not move the bounds (with k = 0.02 /s for every flap angle the tightest values are 25 and 10 too).
# Not implied by the domains (they allow a speed gap of 70 kt and any flap gap of 5.1 deg).
#
# Why the stretch (S), and why it is not exact. At stretch 1 the deployed flaps fall behind the
# reference's without bound in the time-free segment certificate (0.5 deg/s against 1 deg/s), so the
# exact flap rows fail: RELCERT_SYNC=1 DECLINES. At stretch 1/2 the flap motion is matched exactly,
# but the speed is not: the autothrust and the flap drag act in real time, so the deployed spends
# twice as long decelerating per degree of flap, and the speed row needs a tolerance. Per mode: the
# cover uses 1/2 while the flaps move (EXT_L) and 1 once they are set (CONF2_L: both aircraft are then
# the same system, the gap only decays).
# Relation to furnace_program_timebase (the suite's other tolerance stretch). Same skeleton (an exactly
# re-timed program, the flaps here and the setpoint generator there, and a real-time follower that
# leaves a residual (1 - lambda) x its lag), but the stretch is per mode (1/2 while the flaps move, 1
# once they are set, where the furnace re-times every segment), it comes from a hardware ratio (one
# hydraulic motor of two, the grid end point lambda_min = 1/2) rather than a programmer setting, and
# the follower's rate depends on the program through the flap drag.
# How the bound depends on the cause (measured 2026-10-29 on the placard domain, tightest upper
# tolerance against the deployed flap rate r, with the lower one at (1 - r) (F - VLS)): 1 deg/s (no
# failure): the exact claim (all constants 0) CERTIFIES, also under RELCERT_SYNC=1; 0.75 deg/s: 12.50
# (12.49 declines); 0.6 deg/s: 20.00 (19.99 declines); 0.5 deg/s (this file): 25.00 = (1 - r) (VFE - F).
# Law dependence: an arbitrary deployed flap schedule (every left guard the domain, each mode a
# successor of the other: the flaps may stop or move at any angle) DECLINES at every tolerance up to
# 40 kt (measured on the earlier domain). Simulated (0.01 s steps, from CONF 1+F at S): at CONF 2 the
# FLAPS SLOW aircraft is 4.5 kt slower (175.3 against 179.8 kt); the certificate is the inductive
# envelope (25) over every start in the domain.
#
# Domains. f in [10, 15.1] deg (CONF 1+F to CONF 2, plus the 0.1 deg one control period of flap travel
# can carry past the detent before the next decision stops it); V in [VLS, VFE] = [130, 200] kt, the
# speeds at which the aircraft may fly in these configurations: VLS CONF 2 = 1.23 VS1G = 130 kt (the
# lowest selectable speed, the floor the autothrust and the flight controls keep; VLS of CONF 1+F is
# higher) and VFE CONF 2 = 200 kt (the flap placard, the structural limit above which CONF 2 must not
# be selected; A320 family). Both are limits of the aircraft, not of the closed loop (they replaced
# the band [F, S] on 2026-10-29, see Requirement). The placard range is load-bearing and sets the
# tight values: 25 = (VFE - F) / 2 and 10 = (F - VLS) / 2. Every mode's guard repeats V >= 130 and
# V <= 200 (a configuration is selected only inside its placard range), so widening one of the two
# does not move the tight values: V widened to [116, 214] (20 % of the range) in the evolve lines
# only CERTIFIES, in the guard lines only CERTIFIES, and in both DECLINES at 25 / 10 and certifies
# at 32.00 / 17.00 (31.99 and 16.99 decline) = (214 - F) / 2 and (F - 116) / 2, the same formulas
# (measured 2026-10-30). Forward invariant: V' = -k (V - F) points toward F from both ends. Right guards
# partition f (no coverage gap); every left mode has a satisfiable start (equal states).
#
# Stretch-grid dependence. The flap rows are exact only at lambda = the deployed-to-reference flap-rate
# ratio, so the certificate needs that ratio to be a point of the tool's stretch grid (21 points over
# [lambda_min, lambda_max], step 0.025 here; 1/2 is its end point lambda_min). A hydraulic rate 2 % off
# the FCOM's half speed declines: 0.51 deg/s DECLINES (0.51 lies between grid points) and 0.49 deg/s
# DECLINES (below lambda_min 0.5; with lambda_min 0.45 the ratio 0.49 is again off the grid and still
# DECLINES). The claim therefore reads the FCOM's half speed as exact; a measured drive ratio would need
# its value on the grid. (bottling_fill_strainer and furnace_program_timebase state the same dependence
# for their integrators.)
#
# Suite status (batch I9, 2026-10-27): back from benchmarks/reserve/ (batch I8 had moved it there as
# the integrator-stretch template of furnace_program_timebase and bottling_fill_strainer). It is the
# suite's cleanest per-mode stretch: the ratio 1/2 is a hardware fact (one of two hydraulic motors
# on the flap drive), the stretch applies while the flaps move and not once they are set, and the
# speed row is a tolerance claim; it took the place of insulin_basal_cgmloss (M B, in the reserve
# since batch I9; the chain-depth template stays in the suite with tank_dos_3to1 (M I),
# fuelcell_chp_dos, grid_ffr_dos and the satellites).
#
# Verdicts (RELCERT_IMPLIED_CUT=1 unless stated, measured 2026-10-29 at installation of the placard
# domain and the 25 / 10 rows; identical to the batch-I9 pattern): CERTIFIED (about 0.4 s); CERTIFIED
# under RELCERT_NO_PRUNE=1, RELCERT_NO_CHOICE=1, NO_PRUNE+NO_CHOICE, RELCERT_NO_CUT=1,
# RELCERT_NO_IMPLIED_CUT=1, RELCERT_NO_LINEAR_CUT=1, without RELCERT_IMPLIED_CUT, RELCERT_NO_REPO=1,
# RELCERT_NO_PAIR_STRETCH=1, RELCERT_OPREGION=1 and RELCERT_WELLFORMED_STRICT=1; DECLINED under
# RELCERT_SYNC=1 and SYNC+NO_CHOICE. Cover: EXT_L at lambda 1/2, CONF2_L at lambda 1, budget 1,
# admissible start the same mode, joint segments only. Nothing pruned. Rows identical (--handoff
# 3/3, vacuous). relcert --wellformed: EXT ok (exit->CONF2), CONF2 ok (invariant), 0 UNKNOWN,
# 0 BLOCKING. The right model's Assumption 1 proof (WellFormed/FlapsSlowApproach.lean) is the
# batch-I6 one adapted to the placard domain. Letters: S (before and after the domain audit).
[problem]
name = flaps_slow_approach
max_depth = 8
lambda_min = 0.5
lambda_max = 1.0
bound_T = 120.0

[Lsys]
state_vars = [f, V]
epsilon = 0.1
[Lsys.mode.EXT]
ode = f' = 0.5; V' = smt2:(* (- 0.0 (+ 0.02 (* 0.001 f))) (- V 150));
guard = f < 15 and f >= 10 and f <= 15.1 and V >= 130 and V <= 200
evolve = f >= 10 and f <= 15.1 and V >= 130 and V <= 200
next = [EXT, CONF2]
[Lsys.mode.CONF2]
ode = f' = 0; V' = smt2:(* (- 0.0 (+ 0.02 (* 0.001 f))) (- V 150));
guard = f >= 15 and f >= 10 and f <= 15.1 and V >= 130 and V <= 200
evolve = f >= 10 and f <= 15.1 and V >= 130 and V <= 200
next = [CONF2]

[Rsys]
state_vars = [f, V]
epsilon = 0.1
[Rsys.mode.EXT]
ode = f' = 1; V' = smt2:(* (- 0.0 (+ 0.02 (* 0.001 f))) (- V 150));
guard = f < 15 and f >= 10 and f <= 15.1 and V >= 130 and V <= 200
evolve = f >= 10 and f <= 15.1 and V >= 130 and V <= 200
next = [EXT, CONF2]
[Rsys.mode.CONF2]
ode = f' = 0; V' = smt2:(* (- 0.0 (+ 0.02 (* 0.001 f))) (- V 150));
guard = f >= 15 and f >= 10 and f <= 15.1 and V >= 130 and V <= 200
evolve = f >= 10 and f <= 15.1 and V >= 130 and V <= 200
next = [CONF2]

[relational_invariant]
EXT = f[l] <= f[r] and f[r] <= f[l] and V[l] <= V[r] + 10 and V[r] <= V[l] + 25
CONF2 = f[l] <= f[r] and f[r] <= f[l] and V[l] <= V[r] + 10 and V[r] <= V[l] + 25
