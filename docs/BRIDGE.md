# The generic bridge: Theorem 3 of every benchmark from its emitted records

One generic instance layer (`Proofs/Encoding/Emitted/`) turns a benchmark's emitted records
into Theorem 3 (the paper's Soundness of Synthesis) of that benchmark, with every hypothesis
either decided in the kernel or read from one Z3 run (`relcert-bridge-verdicts`). No
per-benchmark proof text: the instances (`InstancesV2/Bridge/`, generated by
`scripts/gen_bridge.py`) are literals plus `decide`. This is the route of the paper's Appendix
B.1; the earlier route through hand-written standalone instances is archived on branch
`archive/legacy-2026-10`.

## 1. What an instance states

For benchmark `b` with IR `p`, extended cut certificate `cX`, cover record `cov` (emitted with
`RELCERT_IMPLIED_CUT=1`; progress certificates, the paper's reposition certificates of Definition 7, by default), progress record `prog` (the same run) and the
bridge dimension `n` (`bridgeDimV2 b`, `VerdictsV2/RunBridge.lean`: the Assumption 1
lemma's dimension, the pack table's `dim`, plus one for the right clock `(Aux, d)` of the
clocked right automaton `rightAutomatonBodyC`; the lemma is lifted by
`Emitted.wellFormedR_lift` under `padOK`, `Emitted/Lift.lean`):

```
theorem b_bridge (hv : Verdicts …) (dt : ℝ) (hdt : dt ≤ εL) :
  windowClaim p n cX cov ⊤ 0 1 2 dt      -- one declared row (32 suite benchmarks)
  keyedClaim  p n cX cov ⊤ 0 1 2 3 dt    -- per-mode rows (18 suite benchmarks)
```

(the auxiliary coordinates: `0` the right mode, `1` the left clock, then the stored left mode
(`keyedClaim` only) and the right clock; the region `⊤` is the paper's unconditional
Assumption 1). A cover with right-only nodes
certified on the restricted mode (the paper's right-only certificate before landing) goes
through `theorem3_window_of_records_pl` / `theorem3_keyed_of_records_pl`, on the cover merged
with its pre-landing record (41 suite benchmarks and both exhibits; `docs/PRELANDING.md`).

`windowClaim`: `∀∃` relational invariance of the declared row with the evolve envelope and the
right mode's region (its guard and kept atoms), over the guard-gated windows of the left modes
and the guarded right automaton of the file (`theorem3Form`, the shape of every earlier
instance). `keyedClaim`: the same over the left automaton of the file with `u_L`-keyed rows
(`psiK`), the shape of the earlier mode-keyed instances.

The hypotheses: `Verdicts` (below) and `dt ≤ εL`. Everything else is proved inside the
instance: the structural bundles by `decide +kernel`, Assumption 1 transported from the suite's
`WellFormedR` lemma.

## 2. The layer (`Proofs/Encoding/Emitted/`)

| file | what it builds / proves |
|---|---|
| `Data.lean` | the benchmark's data from the records: lowered fields, domains, guards, rows (`hostDyn`/`hostEvolve`/`hostGuard`/`hostComps`, the runners' pipelines); the right automaton `grOf` (one mode per declared right mode, one edge per `next` name resolved by `findIdx`, pruned iff the record prunes it, carrying the entered mode's lowered guard); the left automaton `leftAutOf`; the kept atoms; the anchor `AG l q` (row, kept atoms of both modes, the barrier terms of `q`'s pruned exits, envelope); the time model (per-pairing stretch, span `εR/lamQ`, budget); the structural bundle `structOK` and the facts it yields (uniform evolve, sides, name resolution, rows, lowerings, time model, strata permutations, cut-certificate well-formedness). |
| `Stay.lean` | O2 from Z3: each kept atom stays along its mode's flow from the mode's atoms, by the route the certificate records (B/A/C; rational routes re-checked by C) over the evolve domain narrowed by the atom's `given` forms (earlier atoms), lifted by induction on the index (`boxle_of_stays_in`). |
| `Barrier.lean` | pruned exits (Definition 6) from the non-connection pair: barrier `≤ 0` on entry (source check), kept along joint and frozen flows (strict DI on the narrowed domain, lifted by the stays), `> 0` under the target's guard; admissibility (`unsat` of `guardL ∧ guardR ∧ row`); handoffs (the domain-conditioned query); the row's hygiene. |
| `Anchor.lean` | the anchor's hygiene, start, post, switch transfer, preservation along joint segments (`couple_box_cutX` with the pack, the stays, the barriers) and frozen segments (`couple_box_dyn`), exclusion of pruned exits. |
| `Progress.lean` | the reposition-progress premise of every reposition node from its emitted certificate (`progressVerd_one`, `progressVerd_one_down`, `progressVerd_two`, `progressVerd_two_ud`/`_du`/`_dd`) with Assumption 1, the anchor kept, pruned exits excluded; the certificate-shape bundle `progOK` (one stage in either direction, or two upward stages). |
| `Keyed.lean` | the `CoverHyps` of a window; covers of admissible starts decided (`coversOK`, the extended checker on the semantic automaton), other starts inadmissible by verdict; window responses through `coverX_gresp`; `theorem3_keyed_of_records` through `hstepMode_GRC`, `HMode_regionGC` and `theorem3_modeKeyed_gen` (the clocked mode-keyed composition, `ClockedKeyed.lean`; the handoffs are Theorem 2). |
| `Window.lean` | `theorem3_window_of_records` through `theorem3_of_coverX` (rows identical, `rowsSame`); the `_pl` forms of both record theorems, on the cover merged with its pre-landing record. |
| `Transport.lean` | `WellFormedR` transported from the standalone automaton: same modes (`Gr_modeAt` + `rfl`), every standalone edge a declared successor (`edgesCovered`, decided). |
| `Choice.lean` | the chosen sets of the existential mode choice from the emitted choice record (`Trusted/ChoiceEmit.lean`, written by the tool under `RELCERT_CHOICE_OUT`): `chOfC` (the window's `ChoiceData` with chosen successors, chosen exits and hop targets by index), the coverage query of each entry rebuilt from the tool's regions (`leftFacts`/`rightFacts`), `coverage_sound` (some chosen guard holds on the region); the general theorem's `hRJ/hRR/hRS` are the anchor's region facts (`AG_regionF`), its `hcovJ/hcovR/hcovS` are `cov_of_entry`. |
| `Run.lean` | `bridgeGroups`: every query of the benchmark as groups of alternatives (the coverage queries included); `verdicts_of_groups`: a run with an `unsat` member per group is the `Verdicts` structure. |

Axioms of `theorem3_keyed_of_records` and `theorem3_window_of_records`: `propext`,
`Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`, `z3_unsat_sound`.

## 3. The verdicts (`Verdicts p n cX cov prog`)

| component | queries | read by |
|---|---|---|
| `stay` | per mode and side, per kept atom, the route query (`Bridge.stayQueryOf`) | `atomsStayC_L/R` |
| `nonconn` | per pruned edge, the source check and the barrier check (`NonConn.queries`) | `nonconn_entry/keep`, `guard_pos_of_barrier` |
| `joint` | per certified pairing, the flow pack at its strata order and stretch (`modalVerdXQueries`; a component by any of its three routes) | `jointVerd_core` → `couple_box_cutX` |
| `dyn` | per reposition node, the reposition pack (`modalVerdDynXQueries`) | `dynVerd_core` → `couple_box_dyn` |
| `adm` | per dropped start, `guardL ∧ guardR ∧ row` (`Bridge.admQueryOf`) | `adm_sound` |
| `handoff` | per left transition, the domain-conditioned handoff query (`Handoff.queryOf`) | `handoff_sound` |
| `prog` | per progress certificate, its rate/keep/landing queries (`progressQueriesOf`) | `progressVerd_one/two` |
| `coverage` | per chosen set, `region ∧ ⋀_{q' ∈ S} ¬guard(q')` (`coverageQueryOf`) | `coverage_sound` |

`relcert-bridge-verdicts` issues exactly `bridgeGroups` per benchmark at the bridge dimension
and reports the group count against the declared total (`expectedBridgeGroupsV2`).

Declared total: **13677 groups over the 50 suite benchmarks and the 2 committed-subset
exhibits** (`expectedBridgeGroupsV2`), all discharged on the last run
(`docs/CERTIFICATION-CHECK.md`, check 6). The declared count is set after measuring and
re-measured whenever a record changes.

## 4. Coverage

* **50 of 50 suite benchmarks** are instantiated (`InstancesV2/Bridge/`): 32 in the window form,
  18 in the mode-keyed form, 41 of them through the `_pl` theorems; plus the 2 committed-subset
  exhibits (52 instances). Every `*_bridge` theorem depends on exactly `propext`,
  `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`, `z3_unsat_sound`. Five further bridge files (the stretch-0
  demo, the pre-landing demos, the mixed-direction progress demo and the strict exhibit
  `watertank_strict`) are generated by `scripts/gen_*_demo.py` and built by `lake build`
  (`docs/CERTIFICATION-CHECK.md`, check 5).
* **The committed-subset records.** The tool emits the chosen sets (`RELCERT_CHOICE_OUT`; a
  leaf per benchmark in `InstancesV2/BenchChoice/`, empty when the cover needs none), and the
  bridge replays them. Seven of the ten literature benchmarks need them (`docs/CHOICE-SOUNDNESS.md`);
  the Assumption 1 lemmas of the ten literature models are stated directly over the bridge's
  automaton (`<b>_wellFormedR_direct`, generator variant `DIRECT_REGION`). The exhibits
  `vehicle_accel_copy` (a chosen exit set after a right-only segment, hops) and
  `watertank_choice` (a hop) have their chosen sets decided by the extended checker and their
  coverage queries among the verdicts.
* **Reposition certificates in every direction.** A one-stage certificate may run upward or
  downward (`repoProgress_one`, `repoProgress_one_down`, the pin `progressVerd_one_down`); a
  two-stage certificate may run in either direction in either stage
  (`repoProgress_two_ud_reg` / `_du_reg` / `_dd_reg`, the pins `progressVerd_two_ud` / `_du` /
  `_dd`, `certOK` through `Progress.shapeOK`; `docs/PRELANDING.md`).
* **Assumption 1 is unconditional for every instance (`reg = ⊤`).** The chain
  (`coverX_piece`, the progress lemmas, the general theorem, the bridge) is stated for
  Assumption 1 relative to a region kept by every right flow (`WellFormedRIn` +
  `RegionInvariant`; `Emitted/Region.lean` shows such a region survives joint segments), but
  every instance takes `reg = ⊤`, the paper's Assumption 1. The three satellites are proved on
  their declared domain, the gyro box `|w_i| ≤ 1` (`docs/WELLFORMED.md`).

## 5. Findings along the way

* The cover leaves predated the progress gate: `watertank`'s `PumpOff` was flagged a
  reposition node without a progress certificate. With the progress gate (now the default; `RELCERT_NO_PROGRESS=1` disables it) the tool gates the
  flags and the leaves are regenerated; the kernel cover replay accepts every regenerated cover.
* The rational O2 routes (shape, frozen, linear, derived) are all re-checkable by the
  superlevel route C: every kept atom of the suite passes it.
* `decide` (elaborator) gets stuck on the structural bundles (rational arithmetic, string
  prefixes); `decide +kernel` evaluates them in seconds.
