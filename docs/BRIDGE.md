# The generic bridge: Theorem 3 of every benchmark from its emitted records

Status (branch `theorem3-general`, 2026-10-14/15 overnight): one generic instance layer
(`Proofs/Encoding/Emitted/`) turns a benchmark's emitted records into Theorem 3 of that
benchmark, with every hypothesis either decided in the kernel or read from one Z3 run
(`relcert-bridge-verdicts`). No per-benchmark proof text: the instances
(`InstancesV2/Bridge/`, generated by `scripts/gen_bridge.py`) are literals plus `decide`.

## 1. What an instance states

For benchmark `b` with IR `p`, extended cut certificate `cX`, cover record `cov` (emitted with
`RELCERT_IMPLIED_CUT=1 RELCERT_PROGRESS=1`), progress record `prog` (the same run) and the
bridge dimension `n` (the state variables plus the auxiliary coordinates; the pack table's
`dim`):

```
theorem b_bridge (hv : Verdicts p n cX cov prog) (dt : ℝ) (hdt : dt ≤ εL) :
  windowClaim p n cX cov 0 1 dt        -- one declared row (34 benchmarks)
  keyedClaim  p n cX cov 0 1 2 dt      -- per-mode rows (12 benchmarks)
```

`windowClaim`: `∀∃` relational invariance of the declared row with the evolve envelope and the
right mode's region (its guard and kept atoms), over the guard-gated windows of the left modes
and the guarded right automaton of the file (`theorem3Form`, the shape of every earlier
instance). `keyedClaim`: the same over the left automaton of the file with `u_L`-keyed rows
(`psiK`), the shape of the earlier mode-keyed instances.

The hypotheses: `Verdicts` (below) and `dt ≤ εL`. Everything else is proved inside the
instance: the structural bundles by `decide +kernel`, Assumption 1 transported from the suite's
`WellFormedR` lemma.

## 2. The layer (`Proofs/Encoding/Emitted/`)

| file | what it builds / proves |
|---|---|
| `Data.lean` | the benchmark's data from the records: lowered fields, domains, guards, rows (`hostDyn`/`hostEvolve`/`hostGuard`/`hostComps`, the runners' pipelines); the right automaton `grOf` (one mode per declared right mode, one edge per `next` name resolved by `findIdx`, pruned iff the record prunes it, carrying the entered mode's lowered guard); the left automaton `leftAutOf`; the kept atoms; the anchor `AG l q` (row, kept atoms of both modes, the barrier terms of `q`'s pruned exits, envelope); the time model (per-pairing stretch, span `εR/lamQ`, budget); the structural bundle `structOK` and the facts it yields (uniform evolve, sides, name resolution, rows, lowerings, time model, strata permutations, cut-certificate well-formedness). |
| `Stay.lean` | O2 from Z3: each kept atom stays along its mode's flow from the mode's atoms, by the route the certificate records (B/A/C; rational routes re-checked by C) over the evolve domain narrowed by the atom's `given` forms (earlier atoms), lifted by induction on the index (`boxle_of_stays_in`). |
| `Barrier.lean` | pruned exits (Theorem 2) from the non-connection pair: barrier `≤ 0` on entry (source check), kept along joint and frozen flows (strict DI on the narrowed domain, lifted by the stays), `> 0` under the target's guard; admissibility (`unsat` of `guardL ∧ guardR ∧ row`); handoffs (the domain-conditioned query); the row's hygiene. |
| `Anchor.lean` | the anchor's hygiene, start, post, switch transfer, preservation along joint segments (`couple_box_cutX` with the pack, the stays, the barriers) and frozen segments (`couple_box_dyn`), exclusion of pruned exits. |
| `Progress.lean` | the reposition-progress premise of every reposition node from its emitted certificate (`progressVerd_one` / the new `progressVerd_two`) with Assumption 1, the anchor kept, pruned exits excluded; the certificate-shape bundle `progOK`. |
| `Keyed.lean` | the `CoverHyps` of a window; covers of admissible starts decided (`coversOK`, the extended checker on the semantic automaton), other starts inadmissible by verdict; window responses through `coverX_gresp`; `theorem3_keyed_of_records` through `hstepMode_GR`, `HMode_regionG`, `hstep_modeKeyed`, `theorem3_modeKeyed`. |
| `Window.lean` | `theorem3_window_of_records` through `theorem3_of_coverX` (rows identical, `rowsSame`). |
| `Transport.lean` | `WellFormedR` transported from the standalone automaton: same modes (`Gr_modeAt` + `rfl`), every standalone edge a declared successor (`edgesCovered`, decided). |
| `Choice.lean` | the chosen sets of the existential mode choice from the emitted choice record (`Trusted/ChoiceEmit.lean`, written by the tool under `RELCERT_CHOICE_OUT`): `chOfC` (the window's `ChoiceData` with chosen successors, chosen exits and hop targets by index), the coverage query of each entry rebuilt from the tool's regions (`leftFacts`/`rightFacts`), `coverage_sound` (some chosen guard holds on the region); the general theorem's `hRJ/hRR/hRS` are the anchor's region facts (`AG_regionF`), its `hcovJ/hcovR/hcovS` are `cov_of_entry`. |
| `Run.lean` | `bridgeGroups`: every query of the benchmark as groups of alternatives (the coverage queries included); `verdicts_of_groups`: a run with an `unsat` member per group is the `Verdicts` structure. |

Axioms of `theorem3_keyed_of_records` and `theorem3_window_of_records`: `propext`,
`Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`, `z3_unsat_sound`.

## 3. The verdicts (`Verdicts p n cX cov prog`)

| component | queries | read by |
|---|---|---|
| `stay` | per mode and side, per kept atom, the route query (`Bridge.stayQueryOf`) | `atomsStayC_L/R` |
| `nonconn` | per pruned edge, the source check and the barrier check (`NonConn.queries`) | `nonconn_entry/keep`, `guard_pos_of_barrier` |
| `joint` | per certified pairing, the flow pack at its strata order and stretch (`modalVerdXQueries`; a component by any of its three routes) | `jointVerd_core` → `couple_box_cutX` |
| `dyn` | per reposition node, the reposition pack (`modalVerdDynXQueries`) | `dynVerd_core` → `couple_box_dyn` |
| `adm` | per dropped start, `guardL ∧ guardR ∧ row` (`Bridge.admQueryOf`) | `adm_sound` |
| `handoff` | per left transition, the domain-conditioned handoff query (`Handoff.queryOf`) | `handoff_sound` |
| `prog` | per progress certificate, its rate/keep/landing queries (`progressQueriesOf`) | `progressVerd_one/two` |
| `coverage` | per chosen set, `region ∧ ⋀_{q' ∈ S} ¬guard(q')` (`coverageQueryOf`) | `coverage_sound` |

`relcert-bridge-verdicts` issues exactly `bridgeGroups` per benchmark at the bridge dimension
and reports the group count against the declared total (`expectedBridgeGroupsV2`).

Run on the regenerated records: **3512 groups over the 49 benchmarks (46 suite, 3 choice),
all discharged** (suite alone, 3259; the first run before the progress gate, 3657/3657).

## 4. Coverage

* **The choice exhibits.** The tool now emits the chosen sets (`RELCERT_CHOICE_OUT`;
  `InstancesV2/Choice/BenchChoice/`), and the bridge replays them: `vehicle_accel_copy` (a
  chosen exit set after a right-only segment, hops) and `watertank_choice` (a hop) go through
  `theorem3_window_of_records` with their chosen sets decided by the extended checker and
  their coverage queries among the verdicts. `vehicle_hypercert`'s cover uses a reposition node
  (`A0`) whose progress certificate crosses its threshold **downward** (`up = false`); the
  progress lemmas (`repoProgress_one/two`) are stated for upward stages only, so it keeps the
  hand-written general instance (`InstancesV2/Choice/General/VehicleHypercert.lean`, through
  `theorem3_of_coverX`). The mirrored downward lemma is the one remaining piece.

* **46 of 46 suite benchmarks** are instantiated (`InstancesV2/Bridge/`): 34 in the window form,
  12 in the mode-keyed form; plus 2 of the 3 choice exhibits (48 instances). Every `*_bridge`
  theorem depends on exactly `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`, `z3_unsat_sound`.
* **Assumption 1 relative to a region.** The general theorem (and the whole chain:
  `coverX_piece`, the progress lemmas, the bridge) now takes Assumption 1 on a region `reg`
  kept by every right flow (`WellFormedRIn G guard reg ε` with `RegionInvariant G reg`), the
  region carried in the anchor (`AGr = AG ∧ reg`) and in the right region of the claim
  (`cutsR = cuts ∧ reg`). `Emitted/Region.lean` shows a right-only region kept by the right
  flows is kept along joint segments at any stretch (the right projection of a joint run,
  time-rescaled, is a unit-stretch right solution). The suite's benchmarks take `reg = ⊤`
  (the unconditional form); the **detumble family** (`sat_detumble_*`, `sat3w_detumble_*`,
  whose unconditional Assumption 1 is provably false, `*_wellFormedR_false`) takes the
  momentum band `nbF` of its leaves (`*_wellFormedR_onBand`, `*_band_invariant`,
  transported). All six are now bridged.

## 5. Findings along the way

* The cover leaves predated the progress gate: `watertank`'s `PumpOff` was flagged a
  reposition node without a progress certificate. With `RELCERT_PROGRESS=1` the tool gates the
  flags and the leaves are regenerated (40 of 46 changed); the kernel cover replay accepts every
  regenerated cover.
* The rational O2 routes (shape, frozen, linear, derived) are all re-checkable by the
  superlevel route C: every kept atom of the suite passes it.
* `decide` (elaborator) gets stuck on the structural bundles (rational arithmetic, string
  prefixes); `decide +kernel` evaluates them in seconds.
